Proper everyday breathing as the key to a healthy life and good singing

You have surely heard the expression "to breathe with full lungs". It means living a fulfilled life, at full capacity. So what does natural, healthy breathing look like – breathing with full lungs, at their full capacity?

My dears, I will now explain how to begin learning to sing in a healthy way. We will start with the one thing you cannot sing without: breathing. The healthier the way you breathe, the more beautiful and natural your singing.

There is a difference between breathing while singing and the breathing we do every day.

In everyday breathing, the process goes like this:

INHALE → EXHALE → REST

Try it.

In singing breathing, the process goes like this:

INHALE → REST → EXHALE

More on that later. :)

Here is the healthy way. Let's try to make it a habit.

  1. Inhale through the nose.

Why through the nose? By its very anatomy, the nose is designed to filter out dust and impurities with the help of its tiny hairs, so cleaner air reaches the lungs. Its second function is to warm the air, so that it reaches the other respiratory organs warmer.

  1. The belly fills with air and moves forward.
  2. After the belly, the lower part of the lungs fills, the ribs expand, and then the upper part of the lungs fills. Important: the shoulders do not rise.
  3. Exhale through the mouth or nose.

Exercises you can use for better everyday breathing:

First exercise

Relax, straighten up, inhale through your nose and slowly exhale through your mouth. When you feel there is no more air, don't stop. There is still stale air left, which you will push out by drawing your belly straight back at an even pace (pulling it in) while saying "ha, ha, ha" in short bursts until you feel there is no more air in your lungs. You will also feel that your belly is pulled in completely.

This is one way of cleansing the body, because part of the air always stays in the lungs and remains unchanged.

The next exercise is to fill your lungs with air in the way described above (inhale through the nose, fill the belly with air so it moves straight forward, fill the lower part of the lungs as the ribs expand, fill the upper part of the lungs without raising the shoulders). Then hold your breath for 5 seconds.

This is followed by exhaling on the syllable "ha" for as long as there is air and until the belly is pulled in all the way.

Inhalations with essential oils (eucalyptus, rosemary, thyme) are recommended, as they cleanse the body of toxins and mucus. Besides detoxifying, they are also used to treat colds, flu and other respiratory illnesses.

Diet is very important for healthy lungs and for the health of the whole body in general. It is healthy to take in plenty of water, fruit and vegetables and to avoid sugar, flour, meat and dairy products.

Ginger root tea with lemon eases breathing and helps clear toxins and mucus from the airways. Also, eat a small piece of ginger with every meal.

For those who believe in affirmations, here are some meant for healthy lungs:

I have the ability to live a fulfilled life with full lungs. Whatever I imagine, I can also do. I love life completely.

ALWAYS be aware of your breathing. At first, while you are adopting this healthiest of habits. After that, proper breathing will become an everyday – yet priceless – activity.

Yours,Sanja Trifunović Gerginov
