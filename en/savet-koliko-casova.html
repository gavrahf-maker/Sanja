How many lessons do I need to learn to sing?

How quickly a student progresses in singing lessons actually depends on several factors

First factor: How skilled is your teacher? Do they use effective methods, and can they pass their knowledge on to you clearly?

Second factor: How well developed is your musical ear? If you have never sung before and find it hard to hear notes and repeat them, learning may take a little longer than for a student whose ear is already developed. The same goes for a sense of rhythm. But that doesn't have to be the rule if you follow your teacher's advice and practise persistently. Of course, new knowledge needs some time to settle. For complete beginners, developing the ear takes anywhere from three months to a year.

Third factor: How much time do you have for practice? Next to factor number one, this is the most important factor. Once you have decided to take singing lessons, it is very important to be persistent and to set aside at least a little time every day for singing. On a day when you have no time at all, that can be just 10 to 15 minutes. Practising half an hour every day is recommended. The longest stretch without practice shouldn't exceed 24 hours. Of course, it won't be the end of the world if your commitments keep you from practising for a couple of days, but the more days you skip, the slower your singing will progress.

Fourth factor: How often do you have lessons?

A student who has lessons once a week will progress more slowly than a student who has lessons two or three times a week. So if you have lessons less often than twice a week, you should check in more often with the teacher who follows your practice. Ideally, a student has lessons twice a week and, on the other days, the support of a teacher who makes sure the practice is heading in the right direction. My 24 Hours Singing Support offer includes daily support with your practice and a personal written practice guide tailored to your lessons alone.

So, if you have no trouble repeating a note you hear, you practise 30 minutes every day or every other day, and you use 24 Hours Online Singing Support once or twice a week, in two and a half to three months you should be happy with what you hear.

In the end, it all depends on your goals. We can't expect a complete beginner to become a professional after three months. In three months, a very hard-working beginner who practises constantly will be happy with what they hear and will come to love their voice, while knowing there is still more to work on. And a very hard-working student with previous musical experience who wants to perfect their singing can expect to be extremely pleased with the result and ready for new career successes.

I can guarantee this pace for my own work. If you have a different teacher, you should ask them this question, because in the end every teacher's pace is individual.
