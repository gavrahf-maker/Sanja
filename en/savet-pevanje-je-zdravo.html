Why is singing healthy?

Singing boosts self-confidence: when we discover our passion and start pursuing it, we become aware of our own qualities.

In singing lessons we adopt the healthiest way of breathing and holding our body. The way we breathe while singing helps lower stress hormones, creates a feeling of calm and lowers blood pressure.

Singing requires focus and constant concentration; it greatly contributes to a clear mind and improves cognitive abilities.

Singing improves diction and supports the development of the speech apparatus.

By singing correctly we discover the natural beauty of our voice, which is especially valuable for the proper functioning of the thyroid gland.

Yours,Sanja Trifunović Gerginov
