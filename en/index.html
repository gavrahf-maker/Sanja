Meet Sanja

The one requirement for learning to sing is that the student wants to learn and grow.

MA Sanja Trifunović Gerginov is a first-class music educator with 15 years of experience teaching vocal technique. She holds a master's degree from the Faculty of Music in Belgrade and completed further professional training in Austria.

She is the author of several professional publications on vocal technique, and her latest publication, on the fundamentals of vocal technique, is in preparation.
