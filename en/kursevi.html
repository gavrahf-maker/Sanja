FAQ

Before you sign up.

How many lessons do I need to learn to sing?

It depends on your ear and sense of rhythm, how much you practise and how often you have lessons. With regular practice, after two and a half to three months you're usually happy with what you hear.

What language are the lessons in?

Serbian, English or German.

Are the lessons for children too?

Yes, lessons are for children and adults.

What is 24/7 Online Singing Support?

Between lessons you send recordings of your practice, and Sanja sends you new guidance. You choose this option when you sign up.

Are the lessons recorded?

Yes, all lessons are recorded, so you can watch them again.

How do I cancel a lesson?

Lessons must be cancelled at least one day in advance.
